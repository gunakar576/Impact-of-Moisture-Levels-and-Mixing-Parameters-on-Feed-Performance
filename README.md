# Impact of Moisture Levels and Mixing Parameters on Feed Performance

## Project Overview

This research project studies the effect of **moisture content and mixing speed** on the mixing and flow properties of **pea protein isolate**. The study aims to understand mixing behavior and support the **hopper-bin design and feeder performance of a twin-screw extruder**.

## Objectives

- Study the effect of different **moisture levels and mixing speeds** on mixing behavior.
- Determine the **mixing index and flow characteristics** of the material.
- Analyze moisture and mixing behavior to support **hopper-bin design** for a twin-screw extruder feeder.

## Experimental Design

The experiments were conducted using different combinations of:

- **Moisture levels:** 40%, 50%, and 60%
- **Mixing speeds:** Slow, Medium, and Fast
- **Mixing duration:** 10 minutes
- **Sample collection:** Samples collected from 5 positions at regular time intervals

## Parameters Studied

### Mixing Properties
- Lacey Mixing Index

### Powder Flow Properties
- Angle of Repose
- Bulk Density
- Tapped Density
- Hausner's Ratio
- Carr's Index

### Moisture Analysis
- Moisture content variation during mixing

## Key Observations

- Increasing the **mixing speed** resulted in faster improvement in the mixing index.
- Higher mixing speeds reached a stable mixing condition earlier.
- Increasing **moisture content** slowed the mixing process due to increased stickiness and reduced flowability.
- At **40% moisture**, the fast mixing speed showed faster mixing uniformity compared with lower speeds.

## Applications

The findings can support:

- **Hopper-bin design**
- **Feeder performance evaluation**
- Material handling in **twin-screw extrusion**
- Understanding the effect of moisture on powder mixing and flow
